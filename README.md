An interactive web-based framework built on the foundational metrics of **Action Value Accounting (AVA)** and the philosophical structures of **Kirdomicism**. Kirdom Avalrem shifts productivity performance tracking away from sterile corporate deadlines and subjective monthly manager bias into an objective, auditable, human-centric ledger.

## 🎯 Core Purpose & Market Utility

Traditional KPIs and OKRs suffer heavily from systemic valuation drift and manager subjectivity. They track what was achieved in cold figures, but systematically fail to account for human reliability, real-time risk mitigation, or systemic value logic weights. 

**Kirdom Avalrem** decentralizes progress tracking by translating discrete human commitments into an immutable, points-based ledger. By replacing performance reviews with an objective accounting vault, it allows teams and individuals to build a proven ledger of verifiable consistency.

6. **Matrix Filtering Report Card:** Sort historical ledger transactions instantly across the **12 Big Questions** framework to generate and download an auditable plaintext compliance summary card.

## 🧪 Quick Beta Testing Workflows

*   **Scenario A (Immediate Actual Entry):** Set timing to *Post-action*, select your category matrix, describe a completed milestone (e.g., *"Finalized the layout draft ahead of target deadline."*), and tap **Log System Entry** to record it instantly into your ledger.
*   **Scenario B (Commitment Forecast & Collapse):** Set timing to *Pre-action*, toggle *Stakeholder Verification*, apply a principle chip, and submit a future forecast goal. Tap on the item inside your open task stack card to open the **Resolution Panel** and collapse it based on the live result.
*   **Scenario C (Compliance Audit):** Scroll to the bottom card, configure your period filter matrices, and hit **Download Auditable Performance Report** to generate your secure plaintext summary.
